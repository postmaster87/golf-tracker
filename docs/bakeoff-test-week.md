## Every session: the same steps, in this order

**Before. On your phone:**

1. Charge it to 100%.
2. Settings → Security and privacy → **Auto Blocker → Off**.
3. Unlock it, plug it into the desktop, and tell me **"start"**.

I start both apps and check that both are recording, then I tell you
**"unplug"**.

**On your phone:**

4. Unplug it. Settings → Security and privacy → **Auto Blocker → On**. Lock it
   and pocket it.

**During.** Use the phone normally: music, texts, golf-tracker. Do not open
either test app.

**After. On your phone:**

5. Settings → Security and privacy → **Auto Blocker → Off**. Unlock it, plug it
   in, and tell me **"stop"**.

I stop both apps, pull the data, score it, and send you the result.

**On your phone:**

6. Settings → Security and privacy → **Auto Blocker → On**. I will prompt you.

**If you cannot get to the desktop before a session, on your phone:** open GPS
Custom and tap **START**, then open GPS Transistor and tap **START**. At the
end, in each app, press and hold **HOLD TO STOP**, then tap **EXPORT ALL TO
DOWNLOADS**. The next time you are at the desktop, do step 5 and tell me
**"pull"** instead of "stop".

## What I send you after each session

For each app, PASS or FAIL against your bar: no gap over 20 s from START to STOP.
With it:

- the longest gap and the number of fixes
- what the phone's own logs show around each gap: the app not running, or the
  app running with no GPS fixes
- the phone's battery from START to STOP. That is the whole phone together, not
  one app's share. The first number, from 2026-09-14: 90% → 78% over 2 hours
  (n = 1 round).
