# Field card: walking round

**Run both GPS test apps the whole round: GPS Custom (teal icon) and GPS
Transistor (orange icon).** Keep using golf-tracker as usual. The test apps only
record GPS and never touch your round.

They were named Bake-off K and Bake-off T. Until I reinstall them at your next
plug-in, the phone still shows the old names.

## On your phone, before the first tee

1. Open **GPS Custom** (teal). Under BEFORE YOU START every row should read
   **OK**. The Samsung row shows `--`, which is expected.
2. Tap the big green **START**. It turns red and reads **HOLD TO STOP**, and
   **RECORDING** appears under it.
3. Open **GPS Transistor** (orange) and tap the green **START**. It turns red
   and reads **HOLD TO STOP**.
4. Swipe down the notification shade. You should see **GPS Custom recording**
   and **GPS Transistor recording**.
5. Open golf-tracker and play as usual. Lock the phone and pocket it.

If START will not start in either app, leave that app alone, keep playing, and
tell me which one afterwards.

## During the round

- Do not open either test app.
- Do not swipe them out of recent apps or force-stop them.
- Use the phone normally: golf-tracker, music, texts.
- If a test app's notification disappears, do not restart the app. Note the
  hole. That is data.
- If the battery gets low, golf-tracker comes first: stop both test apps (steps
  6 and 7). On 2026-09-14, with both test apps and golf-tracker running, the
  whole phone went from 90% to 78% over 2 hours (n = 1 round).

## On your phone, after the last hole

6. Open **GPS Custom**. Press and hold the red **HOLD TO STOP** for about 2
   seconds. It reads KEEP HOLDING… and then turns green **START**. Scroll down
   and tap **EXPORT ALL TO DOWNLOADS**, then wait for "Exported … files to
   Download/golf-bakeoff/K".
7. Open **GPS Transistor** and do the same: hold **HOLD TO STOP** until it
   turns green, tap **EXPORT ALL TO DOWNLOADS**, and wait for "Exported … files
   to Download/golf-bakeoff/T".

## On your phone, back at the desktop

8. Settings → Security and privacy → **Auto Blocker → Off**.
9. Unlock the phone and plug it into the desktop.
10. Tell me **"pull"**.

I pull the data, score GPS Custom against GPS Transistor, and send you the
result. Then I prompt you to turn Auto Blocker back on.
