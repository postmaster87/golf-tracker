# Bake-off field card: walking round

**Run both apps the whole round: Bake-off K (teal icon) and Bake-off T (orange
icon).** Keep using golf-tracker as usual. The Bake-off apps only record GPS and
never touch your round.

## On your phone, before the first tee

1. Open **Bake-off K** (teal). Under BEFORE YOU START every row should read
   **OK**. The Samsung row shows `--`, which is expected.
2. Tap the big green **START**. It turns red and reads **HOLD TO STOP**, and
   **RECORDING** appears under it.
3. Open **Bake-off T** (orange) and tap the green **START**. It turns red and
   reads **HOLD TO STOP**.
4. Swipe down the notification shade. You should see **Bake-off K recording**
   and **Bake-off T recording**.
5. Open golf-tracker and play as usual. Lock the phone and pocket it.

If START will not start in either app, leave that app alone, keep playing, and
tell me which one afterwards.

## During the round

- Do not open either Bake-off app.
- Do not swipe them out of recent apps or force-stop them.
- Use the phone normally: golf-tracker, music, texts.
- If a Bake-off notification disappears, do not restart the app. Note the hole.
  That is data.
- If the battery gets low, golf-tracker comes first: stop both Bake-off apps
  (steps 6 and 7). Battery use with both running has not been measured yet.

## On your phone, after the last hole

6. Open **Bake-off K**. Press and hold the red **HOLD TO STOP** for about 2
   seconds. It reads KEEP HOLDING… and then turns green **START**. Scroll down
   and tap **EXPORT ALL TO DOWNLOADS**, then wait for "Exported … files to
   Download/golf-bakeoff/K".
7. Open **Bake-off T** and do the same: hold **HOLD TO STOP** until it turns
   green, tap **EXPORT ALL TO DOWNLOADS**, and wait for "Exported … files to
   Download/golf-bakeoff/T".

## On your phone, back at the desktop

8. Settings → Security and privacy → **Auto Blocker → Off**.
9. Unlock the phone and plug it into the desktop.
10. Tell me **"pull"**.

I pull the data, score K against T, and send you the result. Then I prompt you
to turn Auto Blocker back on.
