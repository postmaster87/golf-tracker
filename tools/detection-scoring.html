Shot detection scoring

How often the shipped ranking picks the stops that turned out to be real shots. Ground truth is the confirmed marks in an export; a shot counts as found when a SELECTED candidate lands within 10 m of its mark. Selection is the top N candidates by score in the hole's window, where N is the number of marked shots on that hole — which is what the end-of-hole sheet actually asks for.

Reads exports from docs/roundDownloads/. Those are gitignored (the tracks are a location history and this repo is public), so on a machine without them this page says so instead of failing. Serve with python tools/devserver.py 8123 and open /tools/detection-scoring.html.
